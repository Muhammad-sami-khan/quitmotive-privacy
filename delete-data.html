Delete Your QuitMotive Data

Request deletion of selected app data without deleting your account

QuitMotive users may request deletion of some or all eligible app data while keeping their QuitMotive account active.

How to request data deletion

Send an email from the email address linked to your QuitMotive account to developer.samikhan@gmail.com with the subject:

QuitMotive Data Deletion Request

In your message, state which data you want deleted. We may ask for reasonable verification to protect your account from unauthorized requests.

Data you can request to delete

  • Smoking and cessation profile or survey data
  • Craving records and journal entries
  • Cigarette intake logs
  • Quit plans and quit-attempt history
  • Achievements and progress data
  • AI Coach conversation history
  • Notification preferences and other eligible app settings

What is kept if you keep your account

If you request data deletion without deleting your account, the minimum account information needed to keep the account active—such as your authentication account, user identifier, and login email—may be retained. Any data you do not ask us to delete will also remain associated with your account.

Processing and retention

Verified deletion requests are processed as soon as reasonably possible and normally within 30 days. Information may be retained longer only where required for legal, security, fraud-prevention, or other legitimate obligations. Data handled independently by third-party services may also be subject to those providers' retention practices.

Delete your entire account instead

If you want to delete your QuitMotive account and its associated app data, use the QuitMotive account deletion page.

Questions

For privacy or deletion questions, contact developer.samikhan@gmail.com.
