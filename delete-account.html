Delete Your QuitMotive Account

Account and data deletion request

QuitMotive users can request permanent deletion of their account and associated app data.

Delete your account inside the app

If you still have access to QuitMotive, use:

Settings → Account Actions → Delete Account Permanently

If you cannot access the app

Send an email to developer.samikhan@gmail.com with the subject:

QuitMotive Account Deletion Request

Please send the request from the email address linked to your QuitMotive account whenever possible. We may ask for reasonable verification to prevent unauthorized deletion requests.

Data covered by deletion

After a valid deletion request is verified and processed, QuitMotive will delete the user's account and associated app data under that account, including applicable profile, smoking/cessation, craving, cigarette intake, quit-plan, quit-attempt, achievement, notification-preference, and AI Coach conversation data.

What may remain

Information may be retained only where required by law, security, fraud-prevention, or legitimate legal obligations. Data managed independently by third-party services may be subject to those providers' own retention practices.

Questions

For questions about deletion or privacy, contact developer.samikhan@gmail.com.
