Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 4, 2026  •  Last updated: October 4, 2026

QuitMotive (“we,” “our,” or “the app”) is a smoking cessation application designed to help adult users track their quit-smoking journey, manage cravings, monitor progress, and access motivational tools.

We respect your privacy and are committed to transparency about how information is collected, used, stored, and shared when you use QuitMotive.

1. Information We Collect

Account information

When you create or use a QuitMotive account, we may collect your email address, display name, user ID, and profile photo if provided through Google Sign-In. This information is used for authentication, account management, and app functionality.

Smoking and cessation information

To provide personalized quit-smoking features, QuitMotive may collect information you voluntarily provide, including:

  • Smoking history, cigarettes smoked per day, and years of smoking
  • Nicotine-dependence information
  • Quit date and quit strategy
  • Cigarette intake records
  • Craving intensity, triggers, mood, context, coping methods, and outcomes
  • Quit attempts and relapse information
  • Personal quit motivations and goals

This information is used to calculate and display cessation progress, milestones, cigarettes avoided, savings, and other personalized features.

Financial information

QuitMotive may collect cigarette pack price, pack size, and selected currency to calculate estimated money saved. QuitMotive does not collect banking information, payment-card details, or financial account credentials.

AI Coach conversations

The QuitMotive AI Coach runs on the user's device using rule-based behavioral logic. User prompts are not sent to OpenAI, Gemini, Anthropic, or another external generative-AI provider. AI Coach conversation history may be stored in Firebase Firestore so users can access previous conversations.

2. Camera Usage

QuitMotive may request camera access for the Craving Distraction Game. Camera images are processed locally on the device for facial-expression detection. They are not uploaded to QuitMotive servers, are not stored in Firebase, and are deleted after processing. QuitMotive does not create or store facial biometric templates.

3. Notifications

QuitMotive uses local notifications for reminders, motivational messages, reflections, and craving follow-ups. The current implementation does not collect or store Expo Push Tokens or Firebase Cloud Messaging device tokens on a remote server.

4. How We Use Information

  • Create and manage user accounts
  • Track quit-smoking progress and cigarette intake
  • Calculate cigarettes avoided and estimated money saved
  • Track cravings, quit plans, previous attempts, achievements, and milestones
  • Maintain AI Coach conversation history
  • Maintain user preferences and provide requested app functionality

QuitMotive does not sell users' smoking or cessation information to data brokers, advertisers, or health insurers.

5. Firebase and Google Services

QuitMotive uses Firebase services for authentication and cloud data storage. Account and application data may therefore be processed and stored using Google Firebase infrastructure. QuitMotive's current implementation does not upload user photos, documents, or voice recordings to Firebase Cloud Storage.

6. Advertising and Google AdMob

QuitMotive uses Google AdMob / Google Mobile Ads SDK to display advertisements. Google Mobile Ads may process information such as advertising or device identifiers, approximate location derived from IP address, ad interactions, and diagnostics or performance information.

QuitMotive does not intentionally provide users' smoking history, craving records, quit attempts, AI Coach conversations, or other cessation information to AdMob for advertising. Google's consent mechanisms are used where applicable.

7. Data Sharing

QuitMotive does not sell personal or health information. Information may be processed by service providers necessary to operate the app, including Google services such as Firebase and Google Mobile Ads.

8. Local Data

Some information may be stored locally on your device, including theme preferences, notification preferences, notification history, incomplete survey drafts, offline actions waiting to synchronize, and authentication session information.

9. Data Export and Sharing

QuitMotive allows users to generate a health/progress report containing information such as smoking progress, estimated savings, cigarettes avoided, craving information, and milestones. Reports are generated locally and may be shared by the user through the device's system sharing functionality.

10. Data Security

We use reasonable technical measures and third-party infrastructure designed to protect user information. Data transmitted between QuitMotive and supported cloud services is transmitted using encrypted network connections. No electronic storage or transmission method can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

11. Data Retention and Account Deletion

QuitMotive retains account information and associated app data as necessary to provide the service.

Users may delete their account directly in the app through Settings → Account Actions → Delete Account Permanently.

Users who cannot access the app may submit an account and data deletion request through our Account Deletion page or by emailing developer.samikhan@gmail.com.

12. Children's Privacy

QuitMotive is intended for adults aged 18 years or older. The app is not directed toward children under 18, and we do not knowingly intend to collect personal information from children through the service.

13. Health Disclaimer

QuitMotive is designed to support users in their smoking-cessation journey and provide informational and motivational tools. It is not a substitute for professional medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment.

14. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when QuitMotive's features, services, legal requirements, or data practices change. The “Last Updated” date at the top of this page will be revised when changes are made.
